# Good Habits privacy policy

**Draft, version 2026-10-29.** Written by the founder with AI assistance. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Good Habits is in a private beta.

Good Habits is an iPhone app that reads your Apple Health data and lab results and turns them into nutrition targets and meal plans. It is built and run by an individual developer in the United States ("we"). This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it, and what you can do about it.

## What we collect

**Account**
- Your email address, used to sign you in.

**Profile and safety check**
- Date of birth, sex at birth, height, units, and your phone's time zone.
- Your answers to the safety check (pregnancy or breastfeeding, dialysis or transplant, eating disorder screening questions). We use them to decide whether the app can give you meal plans or only tracks your data.

**Health context and preferences**
- Conditions, medications, allergies, and intolerances you enter.
- Eating pattern, cuisines, foods you dislike, cooking time, budget, household size, meal times, and your weight goal.

**Apple Health data**, only the types you allow
- Daily totals computed on your iPhone: steps, active and resting energy, exercise minutes, distance, flights climbed, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, VO2 max, sleep time and stages, weight, body fat, lean mass, waist, blood glucose, blood pressure, water, caffeine, and alcoholic drinks, with the names of the apps or devices that recorded them.
- **Raw Apple Health samples never leave your iPhone.** Only the daily totals are uploaded.

**Readings you enter by hand**
- Scale readings such as visceral fat or muscle mass, with the date and the app you read them from.

**Consents**
- Which consents you gave or withdrew, the policy version, and when.

**What we do not collect**
- No location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, or browsing data. No analytics or tracking tools are in the app during the beta.

Lab reports are not collected yet. When lab import ships, this policy will be updated before the feature is available.

## How we use it

- To run the app: show your trends, apply the safety rules, and, in later versions, set nutrition targets and build meal plans.
- To keep the service working and secure.

We do **not** sell your data, use it for advertising, share it with data brokers, or use Apple Health data for anything other than providing Good Habits to you.

## Who processes it

- **Supabase** hosts our database and sign-in in the United States. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
- **Anthropic** (the Claude AI model) will process your health data to build health summaries, meal plans, and chat answers, **only if you turn on "Allow AI processing" in the app**. What we send is de-identified: no name, email, or exact date of birth. This feature is not active in the current beta.
- **Apple** provides Apple Health on your iPhone. We never store Apple Health data in iCloud.

## On your iPhone

The app keeps an offline copy of your recent data so it opens without a connection. The copy is encrypted while your phone is locked, excluded from iCloud and computer backups, and deleted when you sign out.

## How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. If you are under 18, your account is deleted when you complete the safety check.

## Your choices and rights

- **Apple Health access:** change which data Good Habits can read at any time in the Health app or in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health.
- **AI processing:** you can choose not to allow it during onboarding. Withdrawing it later from the app is coming in a later update; until then, contact us.
- **Deleting your account:** deleting your account removes your profile, health data, readings, and consents from our database. An in-app button is coming in a later update; until then, contact us and we will delete it.
- **Access and export:** an export of all your data is coming in a later update; until then, contact us.

**Consumer health data.** Washington, Nevada, Connecticut, and other states give you specific rights over consumer health data, including consent before collection and sharing and the right to deletion. We ask for separate consent before AI processing, we never sell health data, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

## Children

Good Habits is for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly keep data from anyone under 18.

## HIPAA

Good Habits is a consumer app you install for yourself. It is not provided by a doctor or health plan, so HIPAA does not apply. We still protect your data with encryption, access controls, and audit logs.

## Changes

We will post changes here with a new version date, and ask for your consent again in the app when a change affects how your health data is used or shared.
